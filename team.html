Mutter Natur Film — The Crew

The people behind the stories.

A small, young team behind the camera and in the edit — united by one idea: bringing people back to nature through honest films.

Camera

Florian Wenk

I'm driven by a curiosity for life and the power of a good story. As a visual storyteller, I shape stories from concept to camera to edit that inspire people and remind us how beautiful and worth protecting our planet is.

Main Editor

Mark Mayer

What drives me as an editor is the craft of the cut. With image selection, rhythm and timing, I build a dramaturgy that creates an emotional connection with the viewer.

Second Editor & Short-Form Video

Balnur Serik

I love the creative side of video editing: testing different visual styles, timing cuts to the beat, and finding the small details that make a video genuinely fun to watch.
